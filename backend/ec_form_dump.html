FAQs

What is a Karnataka Encumbrance Certificate?
A Karnataka Encumbrance Certificate (EC) is the official document that provides a complete transaction history of any land or property in Karnataka.
Issued by the Karnataka Land Records Department, it serves as legal proof of ownership transitions, mortgages, and all registered transactions for a specific survey number over a given period.
Are these official Karnataka Encumbrance Certificates?
Yes. We provide authentic ECs approved by the Karnataka government which are accepted by all banks, courts, and government offices.
What information does a Karnataka EC contain?
Your Karnataka EC includes:
  • Complete ownership history
  • Sale transactions with dates and amounts
  • Mortgage information (home loans, agricultural loans)
  • Gift deeds, partition details
  • Court orders (if registered)
  • Revenue records with survey numbers
  • Registration details including document numbers and dates
Can I get ECs for properties in Bangalore?
Yes. We cover both Bangalore Urban and Bangalore Rural districts — including all areas within BBMP limits, surrounding regions, and developing areas across Greater Bangalore.
What details do I need to provide to get an EC?
You'll need the following:
  • District, Taluk, Hobli, Village Name, and Survey Number
  • For urban areas: Ward or Layout details
  • For BBMP areas: Ward number and property details
How quickly can I get my Karnataka EC?
The EC is generated instantly once you provide accurate property details.
Certified copies (for official use) are delivered in 3–5 working days.
You’ll receive a high-quality document ready for download, print, or submission.
Can I get historical transaction data going back several years?
Yes. Karnataka ECs can show transaction history for any period, typically up to 20 years, depending on the digitization status in your taluk and village.
Do all transactions appear on the Encumbrance Certificate?
No. ECs only show transactions registered in Book-1 at the Sub Registrar's Office.
Not included: Wills and Adoption Deeds (Book-3), and Power of Attorney documents (Book-4).
Are pending court cases shown in the EC?
No. Pending litigation or court cases related to the property are not shown in the EC.
The EC only reflects completed registered transactions.
